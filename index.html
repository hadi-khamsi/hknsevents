The studio

A design studio for the rooms people remember.

HKNS Events is a luxury décor studio in Addison, Illinois. For more than a decade we have designed weddings and celebrations from intimate gatherings to grand affairs, each one built around the couple's style and traditions.

Founder and creative lead Hasan Taqvi started designing events after his sister's wedding, where he saw how much a well-made room stays with the people in it.

How we work

Touch. See. Feel. Believe.

You see the exact pieces before your event, in person, in our private showroom. Nothing on the day is a surprise unless you want it to be.

  1. 01

    Inquire

    Tell us your date, venue, guest count and the feeling you want the room to have.

  2. 02

    Visit the showroom

    A private tour and design consultation. Handle the fabrics, florals, centerpieces, furniture and mandaps that could be at your event.

  3. 03

    Approve a real-life mockup

    See your design built before the day and adjust anything. Or keep a few elements hidden as a surprise.

  4. 04

    Day-of coordination

    A team of two to four coordinators handles setup, lighting, timelines and vendors.

Clients also get vendor savings from over ten years of relationships, and a lifetime referral code to share with family and friends.
