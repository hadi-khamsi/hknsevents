Rooms people remember.

HKNS Events is a luxury wedding and event décor studio in Wood Dale, Illinois. Every event is designed around the couple's own style, from intimate gatherings to grand celebrations.

Founder and creative lead Hasan Taqvi started designing events after his sister's wedding, where he saw how much a well-made room stays with the people in it.

What we design

Wedding and event décor and design across Chicago and the suburbs, for receptions, ceremonies and cultural celebrations of every kind.

  • Stages, backdrops, sweetheart settings and full-room transformations.

    Wedding stage decoration and reception design planned as one complete room, from the backdrop your guests face all evening to the tables around it.

  • Mandaps, Nikkah settings, Mehndi, Sangeet and other celebrations.

    Mandaps and ceremony settings shaped around each family's traditions.

  • Centerpieces, tablescapes, draping, aisles, entrances and installations.

    Florals, linens, candlelight and draping that carry one palette from the entrance to the last table.

  • Personalized design concepts, statement pieces and immersive environments.

    Concepts drawn for your event alone, from a signature backdrop to a room transformed end to end.
