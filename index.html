How it works

Touch. See. Feel. Believe.

Your day should feel simple. Three steps on your side, and we handle everything else behind the scenes.

Step 01

Inquire

Tell us your date, venue and guest count, and the feeling you want the room to have.

Step 02

Visit the showroom

See and touch the actual florals, fabrics, furniture and centerpieces in our private showroom, and shape your design together.

Step 03

Enjoy your day

Our coordinators run setup, lighting, timelines and vendors behind the scenes. You walk into the room you designed.

The studio

Rooms people remember.

HKNS Events is a luxury wedding and event décor studio in Wood Dale, Illinois. Every event is designed around the couple's own style, from intimate gatherings to grand celebrations.

Founder and creative lead Hasan Taqvi started designing events after his sister's wedding, where he saw how much a well-made room stays with the people in it.
